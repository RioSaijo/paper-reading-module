\subsection{P013 --- Smart metering data enhancement in sustainable buildings via knowledge graph-guided graph neural networks}
\label{paper:P013}
\textbf{Authors:} Fang He, Jiaqi Fan, Yang Deng, Xiaoyang Zhang, Ka Tai Lau, Dan Wang\par
\textbf{Major Category:} Sensors, IoT and Data Integration\par
\textbf{Secondary Category:} AI and Machine Learning for Buildings, BIM and Semantic Information\par
\textbf{Keywords:} Smart metering, Building knowledge graph, Graph neural network, Data enhancement, Knowledge embedding, Brick Schema\par
\paragraph{主な主張}
BuildKnowはbuilding knowledge graphからmeter dependenceを抽出し, knowledge embeddingとtime-series informationをGNNで融合して欠損・低品質smart-meter dataをenhanceする. 3実建物で評価し, CV-RMSE 16.53\%を報告した. \cite{He:2025BuildKnow}
\paragraph{新規性}
building knowledge graphを単なるmetadata管理ではなくmeter dependenceの学習priorとして用い, type-embodied representation learningとknowledge fusionによりmeter-specific data enhancement modelを生成する点に特徴がある.
\paragraph{位置付け}
smart-meter data qualityとmulti-source data integrationが主要課題であるためSensors, IoT and Data Integrationを主分類とし, GNNとknowledge graphを副分類とする.
