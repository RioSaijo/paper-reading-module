\subsection{P008 --- Digital twin-enabled smart facility management: A bibliometric review}
\label{paper:P008}
\textbf{Authors:} Obaidullah Hakimi, Hexu Liu, Osama Abudayyeh\par
\textbf{Major Category:} Reviews, Frameworks and Standards\par
\textbf{Secondary Category:} Digital Twin, BIM and Semantic Information, Fault Detection, Diagnostics and Maintenance\par
\textbf{Keywords:} Digital Twin, Facility management, BIM, Semantic interoperability, Predictive maintenance, Autonomous control\par
\paragraph{主な主張}
ScopusとWeb of Scienceから248件を対象にbibliometric analysisを行い, DT-enabled FMの主要潮流をBIM-based FM, AI-based predictive maintenance, real-time cyber-physical data integration, lifecycle asset managementとして整理した. \cite{Hakimi:2024DTSmartFM}
\paragraph{新規性}
DT for FMの文献構造をbibliometricに可視化し, semantically rich lifecycle interoperability, real-time prognostics, deep-learning-aided improvement, autonomous control feedback等をfuture needsとして特定した点に特徴がある.
\paragraph{位置付け}
個別システムの提案ではなくDT-enabled smart FMの研究潮流と将来課題を整理するreviewであるためReviews, Frameworks and Standardsを主分類とする.
