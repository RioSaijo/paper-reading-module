\subsection{P020 --- Ontology-driven energy management in smart buildings: A comprehensive review of methodologies, tools, and challenges}
\label{paper:P020}
\textbf{Authors:} Adamantios Bampoulas, Kaiwalya Raj, Anandhi Parthiban, Mohammad Saffari, Eleni Mangina\par
\textbf{Major Category:} Reviews, Frameworks and Standards\par
\textbf{Secondary Category:} BIM and Semantic Information, Building Energy and ZEB\par
\textbf{Keywords:} Ontology, Building energy systems, Semantic interoperability, Knowledge representation, Brick, SAREF, Digital Twin\par
\paragraph{主な主張}
81件のprimary studyを対象にontology-driven BESを横断整理し, ontologyはstandardised data flowsとmulti-agent coordinationを支援する一方, domain coverage, emerging industry standardsとのalignment, predictive modelling向けtime-series integrationにギャップが残ると示した. \cite{Bampoulas:2026OntologyEnergyReview}
\paragraph{新規性}
occupant behaviour, IoT sensor data, demand response等に限定されていた既往reviewに対し, DT integration, occupant-centric control, semantic interoperability, AI-driven optimisationを含むBES全体の統合的reviewを提示した.
\paragraph{位置付け}
ontologyとAI/ML, Digital Twin, real-time energy managementの接続を俯瞰するreviewであり, semantic modelをprediction/controlへ接続する研究課題の上位背景として位置付ける.
