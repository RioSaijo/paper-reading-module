\subsection{P003 --- Digital twin applications for indoor environmental quality in healthy building research: a systematic review of indicators, methods and computational paradigms}
\label{paper:P003}
\textbf{Authors:} Alessandro D'Amico, Edoardo Currà, Philomena M. Bluyssen\par
\textbf{Major Category:} Reviews, Frameworks and Standards\par
\textbf{Secondary Category:} Digital Twin, Building Environment and IEQ, BIM and Semantic Information\par
\textbf{Keywords:} Digital Twin, Indoor environmental quality, Healthy buildings, BIM, IoT, Systematic review\par
\paragraph{主な主張}
62件のDT×IEQ研究を対象に, 指標, 方法論, 計算パラダイムを体系化した. 環境曝露に関するdose-related指標と定量的sensor-driven手法が支配的である一方, building semanticsとoccupant-centred variablesの統合は限定的と整理した. \cite{DAmico:2026DTIEQ}
\paragraph{新規性}
DT×IEQ研究を, dose-related, building-related, occupant-relatedの指標区分, quantitative/qualitative/hybridな方法, さらにscripting, ML/DL, simulation, semantic graph等の計算パラダイムを横断して整理した点に特徴がある.
\paragraph{位置付け}
Digital TwinとIEQの研究動向と不足領域を整理するsystematic reviewであるため, Reviews, Frameworks and Standardsを主分類とし, Digital Twin, Building Environment and IEQ, BIM and Semantic Informationを副分類とする.
