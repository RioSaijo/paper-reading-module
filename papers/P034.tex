\subsection{P034 --- Automated model generation and parameter estimation of building energy models using an ontology-based framework}
\label{paper:P034}
\textbf{Authors:} Jakob Bjørnskov, Muhyiddine Jradi, Michael Wetter\par
\textbf{Major Category:} BIM and Semantic Information\par
\textbf{Secondary Category:} Building Energy and ZEB, Digital Twin\par
\textbf{Keywords:} Digital twin, Ontology, Data-driven, Building energy model, Building performance simulation, Bayesian estimation, Model generation\par
\paragraph{主な主張}
ontology上でcomponent model applicabilityを表すsignature patternsをgraph searchでsemantic modelへ照合してsimulation modelを自動生成し, operational dataを用いたBayesian parameter estimationまで一体化するworkflowをventilation systemで検証した. \cite{Bjornskov:2025OntologyModelGeneration}
\paragraph{新規性}
semantic modelからsimulation modelを生成するmappingと, operational dataによるprobabilistic calibrationを同一frameworkで統合し, ontology-driven model generationからparameter estimationまで自動化した点に新規性がある.
\paragraph{位置付け}
ontology-driven model generationを理解する上で重要な先行例であり, semantic building informationをBPS modelの構成とcalibrationへ変換するmodel-generation layerとして位置付ける.
